4 August 2023

Lyra & Co. was founded on this day by Keyza Medina Richi Sherlya Fanda, who, ironically, would become the true originator of Lyra. What began as a personal space to showcase her interior design work created with AutoCAD™ gradually grew into something more ambitious. After receiving praise from her school for the designs she presented, Richi began to imagine Lyra as more than a portfolio. She envisioned it as a place where others could learn, explore interior design, and develop themselves through the web.

From that simple beginning, Lyra slowly evolved from a platform for showcasing creative work into an idea built around creativity, learning, and self-development. What started with interior design became the foundation for a broader journey, shaping the identity and purpose that Lyra & Co. would continue to develop over time.
